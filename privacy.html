Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Playso Teknoloji Anonim Şirketi ("Playso", "we", "us"), the developer of Rotate Rescue, handles information in the Rotate Rescue mobile game (the "Game") and on this website (together, the "Services").

1. Information the Game collects

The Game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, photos or contacts. When you play, the following information may be collected automatically:

  • Identifiers: a random install ID created by the Game, Apple's identifier for vendors (IDFV), and a random device key that the Game keeps in your device's Keychain so it can recognize the device if you reinstall the Game. If the Game shows ads and you allow tracking, Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) may also be used.
  • Device details: device model, operating system version, memory, graphics and processor type, screen size, battery level, language, region, time zone and App Store country.
  • Usage data: how you play, such as levels started and completed, moves, boosters used, races, stickers and rewards, session length and settings changes.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance data.
  • Network information: when the Game sends analytics data to our own server, the server receives the IP address the request comes from.
  • Purchase information: if you buy something in the Game, we receive a confirmation from the app store so we can deliver it. Payments are handled by Apple; we never see your card details.

2. Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the Game asks for your consent before it collects analytics data.
  • Everywhere: you can turn analytics on or off at any time in the Game under Settings, More settings, Privacy choices. When you turn it off, data waiting on your device to be sent is deleted.
  • Tracking: if the Game shows ads, iPhone and iPad ask for permission before your IDFA can be used. You can change this later in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

3. How we use it

  • To run the Game: save and restore your progress, deliver purchases and keep things working.
  • Analytics: understand how players use the Game so we can fix problems, balance levels and make it more fun.
  • Advertising: if the Game shows ads, to show them, limit how often you see them and measure how they perform.
  • Safety: prevent fraud and abuse, and meet our legal obligations.

4. Progress backup

To protect your progress, the Game keeps a copy of what you have earned and chosen (such as level progress, boosters, stickers, rewards and settings) in your device's Keychain. If you delete and reinstall the Game on the same device, it can restore that copy. The copy stays on your device and is not sent to us. Resetting your progress in the Game also clears this copy.

5. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only with:

  • Google Firebase (analytics, crash reporting, remote configuration and hosting);
  • our own analytics server, hosted by Amazon Web Services;
  • advertising and measurement partners, such as AppLovin and the ad networks in its mediation, and Meta, if and when the Game shows ads. These partners may receive device identifiers and usage data to choose which ads to show and to measure results;
  • Apple, to process purchases;
  • authorities, when the law requires it or to protect the safety of our players and the Services.

Some of these partners are located outside your country, including outside Türkiye and the European Economic Area. When information is transferred, we rely on the safeguards required by applicable law.

6. How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires.

7. Children

Rotate Rescue is meant to be fun for everyone. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) without parental consent. If you think a child has shared personal information with us, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Türkiye's KVKK, the EU and UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to opt out of targeted advertising. To make a request, email ozcan@playso.io. Please include your device's IDFV if you have it, so we can find your data. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. There are no accounts or forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep basic server logs (such as IP address) to keep the service secure. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page.
